research experience

  • Doctoral Researcher, Carnegie Mellon University and Instituto Superior Técnico2021 – Present

    • Study how self-perceptions of aging (SPA) can be understood and operationalized in HCI research and design.
    • Designed a seven-day Aging Reflection Probe built on established SPA measures and used it with 24 older adults across four living contexts in urban India.
    • Built an emotion-card reflection system in Google Apps Script and Google Sheets with the GPT API. Participants combine card codes to generate a reflection prompt, and the system parses their reflection into emotions. Tested four response strategies: empathic generation, curation of philosophy and poetry, generated fables, and a reframe with action steps. Demonstrated the final version at DIS 2025.
    • Adapted the system into an SPA reflection tool for older adults in India. Replaced the emotion cards with SPA cards and grounded responses in a curated database of Bhagavad Gita verses and interpretations.
    • Worked with a collaborator at IIT Gandhinagar to build the Android app. Deployed it with older adults in urban India.

  • Visiting Scholar, Human-AI Interaction (HAIx) Lab, IIT Gandhinagar, IndiaAug 2025 – Aug 2026

    Host: Yogesh Kumar Meena

    • Developed SPA design materials (reflection probe, empathic reflection cards, SPA aspiration cards) and studied their use with 10 HCI practitioners and 12 older adults.
    • Co-organized a half-day workshop at IndiaHCI 2025: Designing for Self-Perception of Aging in Low-Resource Settings: Leveraging Reflection, AI, and Cultural Contexts in HCI.

  • Research Assistant, MemeX Project, ITI/LARSyS, Instituto Superior Técnico, University of Lisbon2020 – 2021

    • Worked as UX designer for the MemeX mobile app in this EU Horizon 2020-funded project.
