How can self-perceptions of aging be understood and operationalized in HCI research and design?

Self-perceptions of aging (SPA) refer to how people perceive, interpret, and evaluate their own aging. Research in gerontology has linked SPA to health and well-being, and has shown that these perceptions are malleable. Yet HCI has rarely engaged with SPA as a basis for design. My research examines how SPA is experienced in everyday life and how these experiences can inform HCI research and technology design.
