Neeta Khanuja

I am a designer and HCI researcher interested in how people come to understand themselves through technology, the body and the spaces they live in.

My PhD at Carnegie Mellon University and Técnico, Lisbon, asks how self-perceptions of aging can be understood and operationalized in HCI research and design. Before it, I designed for accessibility and wayfinding. Alongside it, I keep a drawing and installation practice.
