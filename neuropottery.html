NeuroPottery: two minds, one pot

Making things is physical. People living with paralysis, ALS or after a stroke cannot throw a pot, and friends, families and therapy partners who live apart rarely get to make something together.

NeuroPottery is a shared pottery wheel in virtual reality. One person’s brain signals control the wheel, the water and the kiln. Another person’s hands shape the clay. Neither can finish the pot alone.

BR41N.IO Brain-Computer Interface Designers Hackathon, 2026 · team Cracked Pots, with Param Rajpura and Harshiddhi Pathak · HAIx Lab, IIT Gandhinagar and HCII, Carnegie Mellon

i.

the mind and the hands

The mind wears a 16-channel g.Nautilus EEG cap. The hands wear an HTC VIVE Focus Vision headset with hand tracking. They work at the same wheel in a potters’ village.

ii.

from brain signal to clay

The EEG streams to a PC. A Python bridge turns it into focus, calm, blinks and jaw clenches, and sends these ten times a second over Wi-Fi to a Unity app on the headset.

  • focusBeta over alpha and theta at Fz, Cz, F3 and F4 spins the wheel from 0 to 150 RPM. Beta stays under 25 Hz to avoid muscle noise.
  • calmAlpha (8–12 Hz) at Oz, PO7 and PO8, as a share of total power, decides the glaze.
  • jaw clenchMuscle activity at T7 and T8 adds 15% water. Focus is held during a clench, so water can’t fake focus.
  • 2–3 blinksAt Fp1 and Fp2, gaps under 0.8 s move to the next phase. Single natural blinks are ignored.
  • calibrationThirty seconds for every user: 15 s relaxed, then 15 s counting back from 100 in sevens.

iv.

Gold bands show where the mind was most focused while shaping. Crackle and kintsugi show how calm it was while firing.

v.

what we solved, and what’s next

We tested the signal processing on synthetic EEG.

  • focusThe focus index rose from 0.08 at low beta to 1.96 at high beta.
  • clenchHolding focus during a clench cut its noise on focus from 0.36 to 0.10.
  • blinks and clayAll 11 natural blinks were ignored. Clay volume stayed within 1% while shaping, like real clay.
  • along the wayVR streaming failed on the PC’s graphics card, so the app runs standalone on the headset. With no LSL library on Android, the bridge sends values over UDP.
  • two handsTwo hands made the clay explode. Exact clay volume, speed limits and resistance fixed it.
  • nextRemote play over the internet, a focus and calm report for each pot, and 3D printing your pot.
