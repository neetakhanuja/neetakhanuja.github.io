AR and installations

Early experiments with augmented reality and interactive installations at the Experiential Design Lab, Delhi: a touchscreen game installation about sustainability, and a web-based AR viewer for apartment plans.

Experiential Design Lab, Delhi · with Girish Singh and Mustafa Siddiqi

i.

a game for ONGC

An interactive game installation for ONGC, built around a story about sustainability. Players followed a superhero through several levels on a touchscreen. I wrote the game logic in ActionScript 2.0 and animated the 2D story sequences in Flash: the opening, the transitions between levels and the ending.

game logic and 2D animation · with Girish Singh and Mustafa Siddiqi

ii.

Avida Towers AR

An early web-based augmented reality experiment for Avida Towers – Global City. A visitor prints a marker from the website and holds it up to a webcam; the apartment plan appears on the marker as a 3D model that turns with it. I integrated ARToolkit with the web platform so the plans could be viewed without special hardware.

interaction design and development · with Girish Singh
